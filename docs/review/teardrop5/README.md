# Teardrop silhouette correction

The previous pull widened the finger side and still looked oval. This patch replaces that radial growth with a full anchored bulb, tapered shoulder and narrow rounded cap. It removes the old skin-pinning mask, which folded in an early tapered candidate. Facial details follow a fuller shoulder; cheeks get a small surface offset and limbs retain their round shape. Physics, camera default, palette and existing recoil/merge timing are unchanged.

`comparison.png` shows the old and new actual browser silhouettes side by side for all tiers. The tier sheets show rest, weak/strong horizontal, both vertical directions and diagonal pulls at 390 × 844 CSS pixels. Detail crops are enlarged for inspection. `actual-pointer-pull.webp` is a new 58-frame pointer-driven recording sampled at 30 FPS; it is not a phone frame-rate measurement.

Validation: 50 Node behavioral tests passed. `character_review.py` passed anchor, multi-direction pull, reversal, cancellation, diagonal release and both merge checks. `recovery_browser.py` passed merge Undo, stable clone-resource counts, gentle motion and Retry. `pull_movie.py` captured the shot without browser/shader errors. `teardrop_review.py` captured 18 before and 18 after native phone frames and scanned 1,839 reference-field points in each of four directions; all sampled Jacobians stayed positive, with minimum determinants from .303 to .351. This sampled diagnostic is not a proof of every rendered triangle or physical volume.

In the cream-pixel image profile for the strong Pip side pull, maximum thickness moved from 66.7% toward the finger to 27.8% on the anchored side; thickness at 80% of the length changed from .593 to .451 of the maximum. These measurements support the visual review, not subjective approval. All three tiers were inspected for rounded tips, attached faces and clear tapered forms. Real-phone feel/performance and the owner's judgment remain separate from desktop Chromium evidence.
