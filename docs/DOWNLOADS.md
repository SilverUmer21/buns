## Run from source

Use Godot **4.6.3 Standard**. Import `godot/project.godot` in the full repository, wait for assets to import, and press F5. In the smaller baseline ZIP, `project.godot` is directly inside its top folder.

An Android APK, real Godot video and updated small project download will be listed only after their actual build/capture and verification succeed. Source snapshots and Blender renders do not imply an APK or completed phone playtest.
