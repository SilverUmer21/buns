# Character feel and board composition research

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for the request to adjust the board angle, retain Pip's appeal, improve Berry and Mango, and add more expressive interactions. The owner's screenshots and the current game are the project-specific references. Other games were not installed or played for this review.

| Evidence | Bunbreak implication (design proposal) |
| --- | --- |
| Game animator Christopher Totten's [animation principles](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/12-principles-for-game-animation) explain directional squash/stretch, material impression, fixed colliders and the input-lag risk of anticipation. | Use direction, asymmetry and overlapping crest/limb movement; preserve stable collision circles and immediate launch. |
| Jonathan Cooper's [game animation principles](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/the-12-principles-of-animation-in-video-games) address volume-preserving squash/stretch, staging, appeal and follow-through. | Use emotion-specific face and pose changes rather than merely widening all bodies. Keep face pieces attached and evaluate at phone scale. |
| Daniel Holden's [spring reference](https://theorangeduck.com/page/spring-roll-call) derives time-based dampers and springs and explains why a fixed per-frame smoothing fraction changes with frame rate. | Use time-based, bounded springs and compare actual 30/60/120 frame schedules. Restore animation pose before applying a single procedural deformation layer. |
| Three.js documents its [orthographic camera](https://threejs.org/docs/#api/en/cameras/OrthographicCamera) and [raycaster](https://threejs.org/docs/#api/en/core/Raycaster). Projection changes require updating the projection matrix; raycasting maps camera coordinates to a world ray. | Offer bounded camera elevation with live preview and persisted reset. Refit the board and project both pointer input and all aim/goal cues with the selected camera. Never change solver coordinates. |
| [Android touch target guidance](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858?hl=en) recommends at least 48dp interactive targets; [game input guidance](https://developer.android.com/games/playgames/input) addresses mouse input. | Keep angle controls and buns selectable with touch or mouse. Device pixels do not necessarily equal dp: verify on actual devices. |
| Official [Clash Royale](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/) and [Clash of Clans](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/) pages show Supercell's own visual direction. The owner asked to consider their color and shine later. | Future research should compare richer contrast and controlled highlights, with new swatches and on-device tests. Do not copy Supercell assets, globally brighten the scene, or change Bunbreak's palette in the current pass. |

The renderer currently uses an orthographic camera at a fixed elevation, a fixed frustum, tier scale factors and a time-based `DoughMotion`. Its core pose is height with lateral volume compensation; crest and limbs spring separately. Incoming buns shrink and the newborn pops, but the owner reports that Berry and Mango appear static and that the squash is not engaging. Camera and apparent size were not user-adjustable. The owner's Pip image is the character benchmark.

Review hypotheses, not established playtest results: a high angle makes faces small; generic squash and shared event timing make variants feel alike; merge removal and spawn need clearer anticipation, arrival and expression. Confirm with captured browser frames and user testing.

Review: capture default and elevation extremes at phone/desktop; verify screen-to-ground mapping, projected aim and gate badge, safe board framing, no angle changes during a held/active shot. Compare each tier's in-game silhouette and expression. Capture weak/strong pull, both directions, release, impact, jelly, both merge tiers and escape. Test positive scales, attached face pieces, approximate body volume, bounded motion, repeat contacts and unchanged deterministic six-level solver outcomes. Human phone playtesting and performance remain separate.

The proposed Clash color/shine exploration is explicitly deferred; the references do not authorize or claim a palette swap.
